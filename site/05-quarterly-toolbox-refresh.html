Chapter 05

5. Quarterly toolbox refresh

Once a quarter, spend one hour tidying the actual tools you use — not the news, not the catalogs: your toolbox. This is the maintenance day that makes the other habits cheap: sources list pruned, tools updated, experimental crumbs swept away.

The quarterly checklist (about 60 minutes)

Block 1 — Sources audit (15 min)

  • [ ] List, from memory, the sources you actually opened in the last 30 days. Memory is the audit — what you reach for is what’s real.
  • [ ] Anything you didn’t open in 90 days: unsubscribe from feeds or newsletters (Chapters 7–8). No sentiment. The stack stays at five.
  • [ ] Add at most one new candidate source this quarter — from a trusted recommendation, not an algorithm suggestion.
  • [ ] Check the feeds still work (a dead RSS feed is silent; open your reader and confirm each shows recent items).

Block 2 — Tools: keep, cut, upgrade (25 min)

For each AI tool you actually used this quarter, ask the three verdicts:

  • [ ] Keep — it earned its slot. Do nothing. Celebrate quietly, move on.
  • [ ] Cut — uninstall or cancel anything you haven’t opened in 60 days. Trials especially. Dead weight is attention debt.
  • [ ] Upgrade — for the two or three core tools: update to the latest version, and skim their changelogs for breaking changes or new features (Chapter 2’s skim applies here too).

Also check:

  • [ ] Your defaults. Are hard-coded model names/versions in scripts or prompts still valid? Update stale references.
  • [ ] Pricing. Any plan changes announced this quarter that alter what you pay or use?
  • [ ] Subscriptions. One sitting: list paid AI services. Anything you didn’t use this month gets cancelled. This alone often “pays” for the hour.

Block 3 — Your experiment log (10 min)

  • [ ] Skim your monthly experiment notes. Any adopted tool from a past quarter now unused? Treat it as a Cut candidate.
  • [ ] Look at the experiment queue. Prune entries older than a quarter and still untested — if it mattered, it’ll resurface in the news anyway.
  • [ ] Pick next month’s experiment now, so the slot is pre-decided. Decision made in advance is a calm decision.

Block 4 — Housekeeping (10 min)

  • [ ] Update your operating system and the apps that update AI-related stacks (bundled assistants, editors, CLI tools).
  • [ ] Check API keys and permissions (if any): remove ones you no longer use, rotate anything stale. Never store keys in files you might publish.
  • [ ] Back up the small stuff that matters: your notes file, your prompt library, bookmarks folder of sources.

Schedule it

Put “AI toolbox refresh” on the calendar for the first weekend of every quarter — or easier, the first weekend after your birthday’s quarter, tax-filing month, midyear month, and holiday month. It’s 60 minutes, four times a year; it has to exist on the calendar or it never happens.

What a good refresh feels like

Ten minutes in, you’ve unsubscribed from two newsletters. Twenty-five minutes in, you’ve cut a tool you forgot about and updated your editor. Fifty-five minutes in, you’ve re-confirmed your five sources and you’re done. The quiet you feel is the noise being removed, not added.
