Overview

Keeping Up With AI (Calmly)

A manual for following AI news without doomscrolling.

There is a way to follow AI that feels like staying informed, and a way that feels like being chased. The chasing way is: refresh X, read three hot takes that contradict each other, feel behind, repeat tomorrow. This guide is about the other way.

You do not need to know everything. You need a small set of trustworthy sources, fifteen minutes a week, and a few habits that turn noise into decisions. This manual gives you exactly that.

The whole guide in five lines

  1. Pick 5 sources, not 50. Trust beats exhaustive.
  2. Read headlines + “what changed” lines. Skip the speculation.
  3. Discount hype words automatically. They cost you attention.
  4. Try one new thing a month. Not one per announcement.
  5. Do a quarterly refresh: keep, cut, upgrade. Done in an hour.

Who this is for

Anyone who uses AI tools and wants to stay current without the dread. Written in plain English, like a friend would explain it — no condescension, no prerequisites.
